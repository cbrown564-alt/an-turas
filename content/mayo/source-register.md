## Sources to review

1. The National Monuments Service lists Breastagh as an Ogham Stone in Breastagh,
   Mayo, in state guardianship (RMP `MA015-018001-`, National Monument no. 415).
   Source: [National Monuments in State Care — Mayo](https://www.archaeology.ie/app/uploads/2025/03/monuments-in-state-care-mayo.pdf).
2. The TITUS *Ogamica* record is CIIC no. 010 and supplies a scholarly transcription
   with damaged/uncertain characters. Source: [TITUS, Ogamica 010](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/ogam/h/og010.htm).
3. The official Irish/English name is recorded by Logainm.ie. Source:
   [Clocha Oghaim Bhréisteach / Breastagh Ogham Stones](https://www.logainm.ie/en/114052/).

## Copy boundaries

- Use the transcription only as a **conventional, incomplete reading**. Keep damaged
  letters and disagreement visible in the historian-approved version.
- Do not identify the people named as particular historical figures, assign a date to
  the carving, or call the stone a grave marker without source-specific approval.
- Dáire, Bríd, the widow, and Colmán are fictional teaching guides and a practice
  inscription. They must never be described as people recorded on Breastagh.
- The app currently contains no source image. Do not add a photograph, rubbing,
  facsimile, translation, or new audio until its licence and attribution are recorded.
