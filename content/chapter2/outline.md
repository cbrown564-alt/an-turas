# Chapter 2 — Oileán na Naomh / Island of Saints and Scholars

**Setting:** Cluain Mhic Nóis (Clonmacnoise), late 8th century — scriptorium racing to finish a gospel book before winter.

**Cast:** Murchadh (master scribe), Cillín (younger monk), Pangur Bán (the cat).

---

## Session 1 — Breacadh an Lae / Dawn at the scriptorium
**Hook tease:** Tomorrow the hours begin to count — and Murchadh will want you to say what time it is.

- Dawn arrival; Shannon mist; bells for prim
- **Tá** + progressive (*ag scríobh*, *ag obair*) — THE present-tense chapter
- Grammar note: *Tá mé* vs *Is mise* made explicit (Ch1 preview pays off)
- Daily verbs: scríobh, léigh, ithe, codladh, obair, meilt
- VSO note preview via examples (*Scríobhann Murchadh*, *Itheann Pangur Bán*)
- Turn: introduce yourself to Cillín
- **No recarve**

## Session 2 — Na Huairí / The hours
**Hook tease:** Tomorrow the pigments arrive from the west — and you'll need every colour name before the winter ink runs dry.

- Recarve: *Tá mé ag scríobh*, *Is mise*
- Numbers 1–10 tied to pages, quires, candles
- Monastic time: madainn, meán lae, tráthnóna; *Tá sé a …*
- Lens: Clonmacnoise → *Cluain Mhic Nóis*

## Session 3 — Na Dathanna / The colours of the inks
**Hook tease:** Tomorrow is a fast day — but the refectory still has opinions, and Pangur Bán has his.

- Recarve: numbers + time phrases
- Ink colours: corcra (lichen), gorm (woad), dearg, buí, dubh, bán
- Scene: grinding pigments (*ch2-inks*)
- Grammar note: VSO — verb first, subject anywhere after

## Session 4 — Bia na Scrioptúir / Scriptorium fare
**Hook tease:** Tomorrow the abbot comes to see the book — and your initial must be finished.

- Recarve: colour words
- Food: arán, bainne, uisce, im, iasc, mil
- *Is maith liom / Ní maith liom* — copula “good with me” returns
- Pangur scene (*ch2-cat*); poem foreshadow

## Session 5 — An Litir Mhaisithe / The illuminated letter
**Hook tease:** (fin) — smoke on the horizon; Chapter 3 Vikings.

- Recarve: mixed chapter review + Ch1 *Is mise*
- Final illumination work; unity theme across the scriptorium
- **Closing sequence:** scene → seanfhocal → present-day (*ch2-today*) → artifact ({name} initial) → fin
- Seanfhocal: *Ní neart go cur le chéile*
- Present-day: Book of Kells, Pangur Bán poem survives
