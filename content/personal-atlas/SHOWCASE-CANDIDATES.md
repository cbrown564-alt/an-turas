# Personal atlas showcase candidates

*Phase 1b editorial queue — candidates, not approved public claims.*

The bundled 80-subject pack remains a foundation index. It gives search and fallback
coverage, but pack breadth is not evidence that an entry is lovingly authored or
specialist reviewed. The first showcase should be small enough that every subject can
carry distinctive writing, claim-specific sources, useful pronunciation, a meaningful
visual or documentary centre, and a strong ending.

## Promotion gate

A candidate becomes a showcase entry only when:

1. every material claim has a stable, claim-specific source and explicit rights state;
2. the relevant specialist and native-speaker reviews are named and complete;
3. pronunciation is useful and reviewed, or the main surface makes no sound promise;
4. its story beats are subject-specific rather than corpus-template copy;
5. its main result states the leading account once and exposes evidence through the
   quiet evidence-mark primitive;
6. a user can leave with a meaningful Irish phrase, story connection, place insight,
   or research path—not only a bookmark;
7. VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, Increased Contrast, and dark mode pass;
8. hard-case testing shows correct comprehension immediately and after 24 hours.

Until then, the entry remains part of the transparent foundation index regardless of
the legacy `authored` field in the pilot JSON. That field describes current content
shape only; it is not evidence authority and must not appear in the public interface.
