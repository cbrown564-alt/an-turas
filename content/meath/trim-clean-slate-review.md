## Evidence notes

- **Grant:** Henry II to Hugh de Lacy, Meath, 1172 — known via later copies (e.g. NLI
  catalogue path to BM Hargrave extract). L1: the king grants a vast lordship; L2:
  copy status.
- **Castle origin:** literary (*Song of Dermot*) + archaeology support early ringwork
  fortification soon after the grant; stone castle develops thereafter (Hugh / Walter).
  Do not invent a single “foundation day” charter for the keep.
- **Town:** early urban community; town walls later; first town charter traditions
  around 1190s — keep secondary to castle/ford unless an episode needs it.
- **Sensitivity:** conquest, dispossession, and Gaelic Meath must appear without
  conquest-as-progress chrome. Multiple voices belong in closer look and at least one
  story beat.
