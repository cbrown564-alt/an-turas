# Personal atlas research protocol

*Operational Phase 0 artifact for `PERSONAL-HISTORIES-FEATURE-PLAN.md`.*

This protocol tests interaction grammar and comprehension. It does not approve factual
content. Run it only with subjects whose claim packets have been reviewed for the
session, and record evidence-language failures separately from interface preferences.

## Three materially different prototypes

1. **Answer → evidence:** short answer, quiet claim mark, forms, ground/distribution,
   human layer, language moment, continuation.
2. **Object → interpretation:** dated record, transcription/form, what it establishes,
   competing readings, short answer, present-day return.
3. **Map/time → language:** present place or distribution, dated layer/form change,
   leading derivation or origin branches, source object, human layer, continuation.

Use the same underlying claim packet and comparable copy length. Do not test color or
decorative variants as if they were interaction hypotheses.

## Session script

Recruit 12–18 target users. Across the sample include two Irish-language specialists,
an onomastician or placename scholar, a genealogist/archivist, and two local-history
or community reviewers. Each participant sees two prototype structures and four to
six assigned hard cases; counterbalance order.

1. Ask the person to enter the name/place without coaching.
2. Observe time to a satisfying first answer and every hesitation around ambiguity.
3. Ask: “What does the app know, what does it suspect, and what can it not tell?”
4. For surnames ask whether the page says anything about their own relatives.
5. For places ask for the difference between the literal words and historical origin.
6. Ask: “Did this change how you hear or see it?” Record the reason verbatim.
7. Let them continue, save, inspect a source, or stop without prompting.
8. Test VoiceOver or an accessibility text size on at least one assigned case.

## 24-hour recall

Contact the participant 20–28 hours later without showing the page. Ask them to retell
one idea, its uncertainty if material, and what the product could not establish.
Record accurate, partially accurate, confident-wrong, and no-recall separately. The
winning grammar needs trust, comprehension, emotional pull, and next-day recall; a
strong immediate preference alone cannot win.

## Decision record

Predeclare the hypothesis and success signal before each round. Put the keep/revise/
remove decision and anonymised aggregate in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Never put raw names,
exact coordinates, or identifiable research notes in analytics or the repository.
