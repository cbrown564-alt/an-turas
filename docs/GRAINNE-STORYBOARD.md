# Gráinne full-story storyboard

*Historical storyboard for the six-episode prototype implemented 13–14 July 2026.
D22 records that this version is too short and supersedes it as the production
storyboard. Preserve it as source and migration evidence; replace it with the approved
eight-to-ten chapter storyboard during Phase 4 of `STORY-LEARNING-REBUILD-PLAN.md`.*

## Arc contract

- **County:** Maigh Eo / Mayo
- **Flagship:** Gráinne Ní Mháille, broader life organised around the 1593 crisis
- **Dramatic question:** Can she preserve her family, authority, and way of life as
  Tudor power closes around Clew Bay?
- **Shape:** six bingeable episodes × ~8–12 minutes; next episode available immediately
- **Completion artifact:** learner-authored voyage chart (Clew Bay → London → Mayo)
- **Next county:** authored by spine after Mayo gold (not chosen here)

The approved first encounter (island → Rockfleet → Gráinne → 1593 letter → *Is mise…*
→ island) is the **opening movement of Episode 4**, expanded by Episodes 1–3 before it
and Episodes 5–6 after it. Do not rebuild atlas chrome to tell this arc.

## Episode briefs

### Episode 1 — Clew Bay

- **Discovery:** the coast is a system (islands, fleet routes, shore power), not scenery.
- **Irish action:** hear and use *farraige / bá / long*; place *as* with origin (*as … mé*).
- **Attention-only ok:** short beats on Umhaill clientship and Atlantic weather.
- **Do not:** catalogue every raid; invent childhood dialogue; lead with “pirate queen.”
- **Quiet collection:** bay/coast note enters Mayo record.
- **Chart seed:** faint Clew Bay outline appears on the voyage chart (no reward chrome).

### Episode 2 — Rockfleet

- **Discovery:** Rockfleet / Carraig a Chabhlaigh makes authority spatial; kin roles make it human.
- **Irish action:** name *caisleán* and *teaghlach*; use *mac* / *bean* in a belonging phrase.
- **Attention-only ok:** second marriage household; Clare Island as related shore.
- **Do not:** stage the battlement “divorce” as fact; invent speech for Richard or children.
- **Quiet collection:** castle place-card.
- **Chart seed:** Rockfleet mark.

### Episode 3 — The squeeze

- **Discovery:** Bingham and Gráinne describe the same coast differently; imprisonment forces the petition path.
- **Shape:** one continuous pressure movement (~8–10 min). Cold open on kin held / livelihood broken; one paired-voice beat; decision to petition. **Do not** narrate 1584–92 year by year.
- **Irish action:** *caill* at the moment of loss; *iarr* as the remaining verb; *téigh* toward London.
- **Attention-only ok:** composition as one background line; Owen only if the learner opens closer look.
- **Do not:** make the learner “defeat” Bingham; romanticise rebellion; dump a full timeline.
- **Quiet collection:** antagonist letter strip vs petition strip.
- **Chart seed:** cracked path inland / broken fleet mark.

### Episode 4 — The crossing & the record

- **Discovery:** the surviving papers hold her name and family in English hands; finding them changes who she is to the learner.
- **Irish action:** *ainm* → *Is mise…*; reuse *as* for self-origin; *tar* for arrival into the record/court process.
- **Source sequence (learner-facing):** petition plea → **SP 63/170 interrogatory and family/maintenance answers as the single interactive climax** → September draft as the royal-response source.
- **Attention-only ok:** Ormond introduction as door-opening; Thames/Greenwich as arrival image without fake dialogue.
- **Do not:** invent the audience conversation; lead with caveats; teach evidence taxonomy; run three scavenger hunts.
- **Quiet collection:** handled letter/folio mark.
- **Chart seed:** London leg drawn.

### Episode 5 — The answer

- **Discovery:** the Queen answers on paper; Bingham withholds full effect.
- **Irish action:** *freagair* / *tabhair* on the royal order; *arís* as the unfinished verb (“not yet done”).
- **Do not:** resolve the whole Mayo afterlife here; do not complete the voyage chart yet.
- **Quiet collection:** Queen's instruction mark.
- **Chart seed:** return path begins, incomplete.

### Episode 6 — Return and the coast

- **Discovery:** she must press again; the coast today still carries the story; later tales remember defiance beside the documentary act.
- **Irish action:** *cósta* on the present shore; reuse place words; *arís* for the second asking.
- **Afterlife beat (affirmative, one screen):** later stories tell of refusing to bow; the letter already holds a defiance—she crossed and made the state answer.
- **Do not:** equate folklore with the September letter; revoke gold with optional extras; open a county picker.
- **Quiet collection:** optional afterlife note by placement, not warning UI.
- **Chart complete:** Clew Bay–London–Mayo; Gráinne's name + learner *Is mise…*; restrained evidence marks only for handled sources.
