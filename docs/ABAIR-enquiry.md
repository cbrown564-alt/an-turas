**Subject:** Commercial licence enquiry — offline Irish-learning app (An Turas)

Dear Ailbhe, Neasa, and the ABAIR team,

We are building **An Turas**, an iOS app that teaches Irish (English → Irish only) through a visual narrative journey through Irish history — from Ogham to the Belfast revival. The product is offline-first: each chapter ships as a bundled content pack, including pre-generated audio for every spoken Irish line.

We have completed a technical bake-off of Chapter 1's 21 spoken lines, comparing ABAIR, ElevenLabs, and Gemini TTS. **ABAIR won on quality for 19 of 21 lines** — particularly on fada vowel length (critical for minimal-pair exercises like *féar*/*fear* and *Seán*/*sean*) and Connacht naturalness. We cannot bundle ABAIR output without your written consent, so the playtest build currently ships licensed ElevenLabs/Gemini clips as stopgaps. The gap between ABAIR and the alternatives is large enough to make licensing a priority for us.

**What we are asking for**

A commercial licence to **pre-generate and redistribute** ABAIR-synthesised audio in the app, specifically:

- Conamara (Connacht) voices now, with an option on Ulster and Munster for later expansion
- Batch generation per content release (thousands of clips per chapter, QA'd individually)
- Non-exclusive terms; we will credit "Guthanna le ABAIR/TCD" prominently
- Clarity on voice-talent underlying consents for commercial redistribution

**Why this aligns with ABAIR's mission**

An Turas targets school-Irish re-learners and the diaspora — groups your funders (An Roinn, COGG) exist to serve. We are pursuing grant funding (Foras na Gaeilge / Údarás) and a licensing agreement that keeps public Irish-language investment in the ecosystem would strengthen both our applications.

**What we can show you**

A working SwiftUI prototype with all five Chapter 1 sessions, including the bake-off results (`tools/tts-bakeoff/winners.json`). We would welcome a call to discuss scope, fee structure, and timeline.
