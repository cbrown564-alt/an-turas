# Personal atlas editorial method and corrections policy

## Publication unit

The publication unit is a material assertion, not a page. Every assertion requires a
neutral statement, scope, evidence reference, certainty, named reviewer, review date,
rights state, and explicit competing assertions where relevant. A page with many
sources can still fail if one material sentence is not traceable.

## Release progression

`showcase candidate → specialist reviewed → showcase`

The content-review tool enforces this progression. `authored` and `foundation` describe
content shape only. They never confer authority. Advancement requires claim decisions,
named discipline review, rights, resolved audio state, and accessibility sign-off.

## Names

- Preserve entered casing and display forms while matching diacritic-insensitively.
- Model Ó/O’, Mac/Mc, Ní/Nic, translation, anglicisation, and historic spelling as
  typed relationships rather than interchangeable strings.
- Separate ultimate origin, documented life in Ireland, record distribution, and a
  particular family line.
- Never infer descent, clan, crest, migration, relationship, character, or genetics.
- Multiple origins stay as branches unless evidence scopes them more narrowly.

## Places

- Give Irish and English/current forms equal editorial dignity.
- Keep literal gloss, proposed derivation, referent, and tradition separate in data.
- Disambiguate duplicate names with county, place type, hierarchy, and map context.
- Northern Ireland terminology and contested names require appropriate community and
  discipline review; the interface cannot settle a civic naming dispute by typography.

## Pronunciation and media

Only a verified human/native-speaker recording may be presented as authoritative for
a personal name. An approximate guide is labelled as such. Absence is a valid state;
decorative audio, map, image, or generated prose is not a fallback for missing rights.

## Corrections

“Another local form,” “wrong place,” and factual correction are private editorial
leads. A submission records subject/assertion context and an optional source. It never
changes public content automatically and is never decided by vote. The editor either
links it to an existing assertion, opens a competing assertion, requests evidence, or
closes it with a reason; the audit trail remains durable.

## Privacy

Raw names, genealogy queries, exact locations, worksheet content, and correction text
are excluded from product analytics. The query ledger accepts only published subject
IDs, coarse unresolved reasons, ambiguity/continuation outcomes, and timestamps. Near-
me rounds a one-time device fix to a coarse area and does not persist it.
