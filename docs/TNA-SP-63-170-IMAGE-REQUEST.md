# TNA SP 63/170 image request

## Required images

- `SP 63/170 f. 201`: first page of the eighteen articles of interrogatory; primary
  Episode 4 name-find image.
- `SP 63/170 f. 202`: page of Gráinne's answers with Lord Burghley's marginal notes;
  secondary evidence image.

The National Archives' former blog article confirms these folio functions. The 1890
*Calendar of State Papers relating to Ireland*, volume 5, entries 62–63, renders the
heading as “Grany Ne Malley” and the answer heading as “Grany Ne Maelly.”

## Current educational-use decision

The TNA web-resolution `f. 201` scan is bundled while the app is entirely free,
exclusively educational and has no commercial involvement. TNA permits educational use
of copies of Crown copyright records. The app preserves the source image without
colourisation or destructive editing and displays the repository and catalogue credit
beside it.
