# Editorial board — recruitment pack

*12 July 2026 · materials to recruit the minimum board required by STATUS / D9 / D12.
This does not complete recruitment; it makes outreach ready.*

## Minimum seats

| Seat | Must cover | Immediate work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irish-language pedagogue | Connacht-first forms, TEG fit, 20-word weaves, speech UX | Clear Mayo 20; advise Offaly/Dublin weaves |

Optional later: numismatist (Dublin), art historian (Offaly cross), audio native-speaker
QA (can be the pedagogue or a separate paid reviewer).

## Role cards

### Irish-language pedagogue

- **Time:** ~4–6 hours on Mayo weave + constructions; then per-county reviews.
- **Deliverable:** approve or amend the 20 headwords; mark mutations/audio risks;
  reject calques; confirm listening-first moments.
- **Not asked:** to invent history or write SwiftUI.
- **Working docs:** `docs/GRAINNE-LANGUAGE-WEAVE.md`, county briefs, draft JSON when it exists.

### Historian

- **Time:** ~6–10 hours on Mayo claim ledger + L1 paraphrases first.
- **Deliverable:** tick/amend claim ledger; approve story-view paraphrases; set
  closer-look uncertainty bounds; flag rights-sensitive quotations.
- **Not asked:** to write learner pedagogy or ship audio.
- **Working docs:** `content/mayo/grainne-1593-source-brief.md` and sibling briefs.
