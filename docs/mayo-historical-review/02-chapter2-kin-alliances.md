## Claim-by-claim analysis

### C05 — Marriages: Dónal an Chogaidh Ó Flaithbheartaigh; later Risdeárd an Iarainn Bourke

| Field | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger certainty | **story** (retain), with chronology soft |
| Primary spine | July 1593 answers name the first husband and treat her as widow of a second (“last husband”); Sept 1593 draft speaks of “her two husbands’ lands” |
| Secondary | Near-universal: O’Flaherty first, Bourke second |

**What the 1593 materials actually support (high confidence for fact of two marriages; medium for exact English spellings until diplomatic check):**

- Healy’s paraphrase of her answers (based on State Papers, July 1593): first husband called **Donall “Ichoggy” O’Flaherty**, chieftain of Ballynahinch during his life; after his death, Teige O’Flaherty seized Ballynahinch against the widow and her sons.
- Same answer tradition names sons of that marriage as **Owen** and **Morogh** (Healy, *Irish Essays*, “her children”).
- Answer to maintenance after the last husband’s death (Chambers quotation via Scoilnet / secondary teaching packs; also NML paraphrase): after that death she gathered followers and cattle and **“became a dweller in Carrigahowley.”**
- Cecil Papers draft, 6 Sept 1593: Elizabeth to Bingham — Grany ne Maly seeks favour for sons **Morogh O’Flaharty** and **Tibbott Burk**, and brother **Donell O’Piper**; notes she has, by Irish custom, no title to livelihood from **“her two husbands’ lands.”**

**What is secondary synthesis, not a dated line in the papers:**

| Event | Common secondary range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First marriage | often **c. 1546** (Murray; Chambers timeline) | Not a year recovered from the 1593 answers. NML popular piece even says first husband’s death “in the 1550s,” against the more usual c. 1564–65 death date — proof that year precision is contested. |
| First husband’s death | often **c. 1564–65** (Murray: Joyce skirmish after Murrough-na-dTuadh deal) | Contested edge; not needed for Ch2 L1. |
| Second marriage | **1566** (Heritage Ireland Rockfleet page; many popular accounts) or **1567** (Murray *History Ireland*; NML) | DIB path for Risdeárd an Iarainn: marriage **about 1566–7**. Do not pick one year on L1. |
| Second husband’s death | **April 1583** (Murray; DIB “shortly before 15 April 1583”; NML) vs **1584** on some Heritage Ireland copy | Prefer **1583** in L2/L3; on L1 say only that she was later a widow of the Bourke marriage. |

**1593 vs secondary synthesis rule for C05:**  
L1 may assert **two marriages, O’Flaherty then Bourke, connecting her to two Connacht houses**. L1 must **not** assert “married in 1546 / 1566 / 1567” or stage domestic scenes. The draft’s marriages page already states the right hedge.

**Confidence:** High that both marriages are documentary; medium that Irish scholarly name-forms are the right learner-facing gloss of period English; low for any exact wedding year on L1.

## Marriage chronology bounds for L1

**Allowed on L1 (Story + Learning narrative):**

- Two marriages: **O’Flaherty, then Bourke**.
- They joined houses / territories / obligations.
- Exact dates are uncertain; later divorce tales are not treated as fact.
- After the Bourke husband’s death she was associated with Rockfleet (Carrigahowley) as a dwelling base.

**Blocked on L1:**

- Fixed years: 1546, 1566, 1567, 1580 move to Lough Mask, etc.
- Battlement dismissal / one-year provisional marriage as history.
- Invented wedding or household dialogue.
- “Divorced then remarried the same man” as fact.

**L2 / L3 may say:**

- First marriage traditionally dated **c. mid-1540s** (often 1546); death of Dónal often **c. 1564–65** — secondary.
- Second marriage **c. 1566–67**; Richard’s death **April 1583** (prefer over 1584); she describes becoming a dweller at Carrigahowley thereafter.
- Cite: Murray (*History Ireland*); Chambers; Cecil draft for “two husbands”; answer paraphrase for Ichoggy / Carrigahowley.
