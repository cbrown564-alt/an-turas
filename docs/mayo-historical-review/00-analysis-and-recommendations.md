## What the research established

### C04 is no longer an unknown citation

Sidney’s recognition is **primary**, not secondary guesswork:

- Text: “most famous feminine sea captain… **three galleys and two hundred
  fighting men**…” (name form **Grany Imallye**).
- Document: memoir to Walsingham, Ludlow, **1 March 1582/3**, recalling a
  **1576** Galway meeting during the third deputyship.
- Trail: Lambeth **Carew MS 601** (calendar vol. II doc. 501) and TNA
  **SP 12/159** (publicly cited **f. 27d**).

**Do not** cite as a 1577 letter to Philip Sidney (common Wikipedia error).
**Do not** promote Murray’s Galway harbour-sail / billing detail without a
separate primary. Exact three/200 figures belong in L2–L3; L1 can keep “galleys
and fighting men” or promote the numbers once the cite is on the page.

draft is explicit).

### Chapter 2 / 4 chapter weight is supportable

- **C05–C07:** two marriages (O’Flaherty then Bourke) and Rockfleet as
  Bourke-associated later base are documentary; wedding years and Heritage
  Ireland move-dates stay off L1. L1 children = crisis kin (Tibbott, brother
  Donal); Owen / Murrough / Méadhbh = L2.
- **C03:** petition language of maintaining herself and people **by sea and
  land** / “trade of maintenance” carries Ch4; tolling is scholarly + hostile
  synthesis — keep as interpretive frame, not her verbatim self-label.
- **C08** in Ch4 = vulnerability seed only; full Bingham arc stays in Ch5.

C16–C18 reception/exclude. Prefer “she had to **ask again**” for 1595 over “no
