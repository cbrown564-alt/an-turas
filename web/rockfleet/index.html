Rockfleet at the tide line.

An Turas teaches Irish through the real places, people, and records that gave the language its shape. This first public preview stays with one question: how could one place hold boats, family, livelihood, and authority together?

One place. One account. A little Irish.

This is a portfolio exhibit, not the complete 32-county course. It shows the editorial and learning loop that the native iOS experience is built to carry further.

01 · Place and person

The coast is part of the story.

Rockfleet, or Carraig a Chabhlaigh, sits beside Clew Bay. In the surviving record, Gráinne Ní Mháille is associated with this maritime world: boats, shore castles, family, followers, and the sea routes that made authority possible.

Rockfleet · an orientation mark, not a historical map

The documentary spine is not a complete biography. It is a set of encounters: a petition, questions and answers, royal instructions, hostile official letters, and later pressure that did not simply disappear.
