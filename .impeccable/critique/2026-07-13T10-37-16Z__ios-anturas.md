## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does it look AI-generated?** Not as a product concept. The historical specificity, evidence discipline, mineral palette, and refusal of gamified Irishness feel authored. The Gráinne story has a genuine point of view.

Localized implementation tells remain: 49 uses of the small tracked `Eyebrow`, repeated tonal cards, certainty pills, abstract placeholder art, and a broad atlas shell that exposes the product model before the content earns it. The story is more convincing than the shell.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json ios/AnTuras` returned `[]` with exit code 0 because it scans HTML/CSS, not SwiftUI. This is a tooling limitation, not a clean bill of health. The native pass inspected 39 Swift files / 14,613 lines and found 377 fixed-size font calls, zero `@ScaledMetric`, zero `.dynamicTypeSize`, no Increased Contrast handling, 71 animation calls against 22 Reduce Motion declarations, and multiple sub-44-point controls.

**Visual overlays:** not applicable to a native SwiftUI target. No browser overlay or live server was used. Simulator inspection was used instead.

## Overall Impression

An Turas has a rare thing: a differentiated product thesis that is already visible in the interface. Rockfleet → Gráinne → the letter → “Is mise…” is focused, emotionally coherent, and much more memorable than a conventional language lesson.

The largest opportunity is to protect that intimacy. Today, continuity breaks at step transitions and relaunch; the core pronunciation is unavailable; the crucial record is represented by a pseudo-manuscript; and the app then expands into more atlas architecture than one completed encounter can support. This is a strong design prototype, not yet a trustworthy release candidate.

### Cognitive load

The four-step story is mostly low-load. The atlas and long personal-result tail are high-load, failing single focus, visual hierarchy, minimal choices, working-memory support, and progressive disclosure.

- Screenshot 10 exposes at least 10 choices before county targets: search, two personal hooks, three map modes, and four tabs.
- Screenshot 11 still shows six major choices, while static road rows look tappable.
- Screenshot 13 presents eight actions after a long scroll: two saves, keepsake, field/offline mode, share, sources, correction, and methodology.
- Screenshots 4, 7, and 9 begin with their main heading already clipped, forcing users to reconstruct the new step from partial context.

### Emotional journey

The intended movement is excellent: curiosity → stakes at Rockfleet → recognition of Gráinne as a person → discovery in the record → agency through self-identification → belonging on the island.

The peak is Screenshot 7, where “Grany ne Maly” becomes “Gráinne Ní Mháille.” Evidence, identity, and language meet in one moment. Screenshot 9 is a good ending because the learner leaves with a usable sentence. The experience then drops into a valley: unavailable audio weakens the confidence payoff, and Screenshot 10 replaces the intimate close with a dense system map.

## What’s Working

1. **The core story enacts the product thesis.** Evidence changes the narrative rather than decorating it, and Irish arrives because names matter.
2. **The visual identity is specific.** Limestone, moss, lichen, editorial serif moments, and native iOS chrome avoid both tourist-green cliché and generic language-app gamification.
3. **The personal atlas points toward the right evidence model.** Screenshot 12 gives the answer first, then a quiet 44-point evidence mark, older forms, derivation, map, sources, corrections, and limits on demand. Its semantic type and evidence control should be the implementation model for the older atlas surfaces.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Journey continuity breaks between steps and across launches

**Why it matters:** Screenshots 4 and 7 open at “the record.” and Screenshot 9 clips “A name can cross centuries.” The story `ScrollView` survives step changes without returning to its top. More seriously, `hasOpenedAtlas`, `storyCompleted`, evidence, field-note, and return state live only in `AtlasPrototypeModel`; a force-quit resets the carried journey.

**Fix:** Add an explicit top anchor with `ScrollViewReader` and reset after each step transition. Persist the current story step and atlas outcomes through `AppState`/durable storage. Return interrupted learners to a clear “Continue your story” state.

**Evidence:** [MayoStoryPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/MayoStoryPrototype.swift:314), [AtlasPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/AtlasPrototype.swift:171)

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] The current atlas violates its Dynamic Type, contrast, touch-target, and Reduce Motion contract

**Why it matters:** The native pass found 377 fixed-size font calls, no practical Dynamic Type infrastructure, and a first-run screen that was pixel-identical at Large and Accessibility XXXL. Screenshot 6 already truncates Gráinne’s name at default size. In light mode, `inkFaint` and `atlasGold` are 2.76:1 on limestone, lichen is 3.81:1, and atlas green is 4.26:1. Several map labels are 7.5–10.5 pt; inline personal hooks and theme chips are below 44 pt. Shared press/shake motion ignores Reduce Motion.

**Fix:** Move Mayo/atlas text to semantic styles, introduce scaled display metrics only where needed, allow names to wrap, and adapt side-by-side layouts at accessibility sizes. Darken essential metadata tokens, add an Increased Contrast palette, enforce 44×44 targets and selection traits, and make shared motion reduce-aware. Give the accessible map alternative the same route/time/theme meaning.

**Evidence:** [Theme.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/Theme.swift:8), [AtlasPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/AtlasPrototype.swift:257), [IslandAtlasView.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/IslandAtlasView.swift:243)

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The language-and-evidence promise is visibly unfinished at its most important moments

**Why it matters:** Screenshots 2, 8, and 9 make pronunciation a hero and then say “Audio coming soon.” No audio media are bundled, so the core hinge—hearing and saying “Is mise…”—cannot deliver its promise. Screenshots 4 and 7 show a blank pseudo-manuscript that can be mistaken for the surviving record, while five illustration files are actually JPEGs named `.png` and are omitted from the successful build.

**Fix:** Do not release the opening encounter without reviewed audio for Gráinne’s name and “Is mise…”. If audio is unavailable in development, render a clearly noninteractive pronunciation guide. Replace the pseudo-manuscript with a sourced transcription/excerpt or clearly labeled explanatory diagram and an obvious path to provenance. Correct the asset formats/names and make missing production media fail in CI, not in learner copy.

**Evidence:** [Speech.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/Speech.swift:38), [AtlasPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/AtlasPrototype.swift:322), [MayoStoryPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/MayoStoryPrototype.swift:567), [SessionView.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/SessionView.swift:346)

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] Evidence semantics and copy invert the intended hierarchy

**Why it matters:** The crucial story facsimile is under-labeled, while peripheral surfaces over-explain taxonomy through `DOCUMENTED` pills and “CLAIMS THIS PROTOTYPE DOES NOT MAKE.” That makes the learner-facing voice institutional and defensive. “Pilot excerpt,” “accessible county list,” and “Take this place offline” similarly expose production language or overstate behavior.

**Fix:** Give the story object one quiet but unmistakable explanatory-facsimile mark with a 44-point source-detail action. Remove routine certainty pills from ordinary factual prose. Reserve persistent labels for reconstruction, dispute, tradition, or unknowns. Rewrite the warning block as affirmative “What the record carries / What later stories remember,” and replace implementation-facing labels with learner language.

**Evidence:** [MayoStoryPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/MayoStoryPrototype.swift:173), [MayoStoryPrototype.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/MayoStoryPrototype.swift:651)

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P2] The post-story information architecture exposes more product than the content earns

**Why it matters:** After one intimate encounter, Screenshots 10–11 reveal four tabs, three map modes, personal search, all counties, an opening road, a field note, and collection/return systems. Unready counties are tappable and described as open to inspect. Static cards and road stops look actionable. This contradicts the project’s own approved principle that first run is authored and the four-tab atlas is not yet validated.

**Fix:** Make one post-story action inevitable: continue the authored road or open the completed Mayo record. Delay Time/Theme and secondary destinations until they contain real material. Keep future counties visible as context but clearly noninteractive. Distinguish editorial information rows from tappable surfaces.

**Evidence:** [IslandAtlasView.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/IslandAtlasView.swift:130), [IslandAtlasView.swift](/Users/cobro/code/irish/ios/AnTuras/IslandAtlasView.swift:610)

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

### Jordan — first-time learner

- New story steps open mid-heading, so the reason for the transition is missing.
- Irish-only tab labels and the `Ar Ais` back-arrow metaphor require interpretation.
- Audio looks actionable but is disabled, with no explanation beyond “coming soon.”
- Static atlas cards look like routes, while the next intended action is not singular.

### Sam — VoiceOver, low vision, or accessibility text

- The opening screen did not change between Large and Accessibility XXXL in simulator evidence.
- Essential small metadata uses low-contrast tokens and sub-11-point text.
- Compact personal hooks and theme chips miss 44-point targets; theme selection lacks an accessible selected trait.
- The map is appropriately hidden, but the county-list alternative does not communicate its route/date/theme/story signals.
- Raw drag tracing and some sliders need equivalent names, values, and non-gesture feedback verified on device.

### Casey — distracted, one-handed mobile learner

- Bottom story actions are well placed, but interruption or relaunch can erase the exact step and completion state.
- Small inline hooks and county-outline tapping demand precision.
- The personal-result tail exposes eight actions after a long scroll.
- A long map precedes the accessible list, pushing the reliable alternative out of the immediate thumb zone.

### Aoife — adult returner living abroad

- Missing pronunciation recreates the school experience of seeing Irish without confidence to say it.
- Caveat-heavy “prototype” language turns an intimate encounter into an institutional explanation.
- Losing progress contradicts the emotional promise that words and evidence are carried.
- The Belfast personal result is a strong all-island signal, but the audio/dialect experience must broaden before a Northern Ireland launch.

## Minor Observations

- The simulator build succeeded on Xcode 26.6 / iPhone 16e; this is not a build-stability critique.
- Five art files (`s3-yard`, `t1-pillar`, `t2-hands`, `t3-strand`, `t4-today`) are JPEG data with `.png` names and are omitted by `pngcrush`.
- The collection says “5 OF 20” and that the production story “would earn” twenty—useful internal truth, unfinished learner copy.
- Journey `RoadStop` completion/active/ahead state is color-only and lacks accessible state text.
- The disabled “Use your first Irish” action does not say “Find Gráinne’s name first.”
- “Take this place offline” currently opens a bundled field-mode view; rename it or show a real download state.
- The toolbar bookmark and labeled save are defensible redundancy, but save confirmation should be announced.
- The abstract portrait is responsibly labeled interpretive, yet it makes the headline person feel less human than the copy.
- Personal search is a strong implementation exception: it has invitation, loading, no-match, ambiguity, error, privacy, and VoiceOver announcements.
- No high-confidence performance blocker was found; the 51 MB personal corpus stays SQLite-backed and search is actor-isolated.
