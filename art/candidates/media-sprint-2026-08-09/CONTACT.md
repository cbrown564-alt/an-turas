## Calibration decision

The medium and restrained palette are repeatable. Landscape and distant-settlement
prompts are productive. Elevated archaeological reconstruction prompts make the model
produce clean diagrams and should not be scaled without a viewpoint change. People,
costume, documents, inscriptions, named likenesses, and evidence objects stay out of
the bulk queue unless a source-backed brief and separate review route exist.

Motion generation was not callable from this project's local task environment, but
that was a local-access limitation, not a provider blocker. Central orchestration has
since proved that Gemini web Video mode completes 10-second jobs. Selected stills are
therefore queued for central Gemini submission. Temporary service errors should be
retried as bounded provider failures; any returned audio must be removed during the
muted delivery encode. No synthetic pan/zoom substitute will be created.
