The Stones of Names — Maigh Eo, c. 480 AD

Before Ireland wrote on vellum, she wrote on stone — names, carved in strokes along an edge, meant to outlast the people who spoke them. In this chapter you learn Irish the way it first survived: one name at a time. Five short sessions. Every correct answer carves a stroke.
